Published

Cross-platform deployment and characterization of an on-device generative conversational agent

P. Firpo, R. Berta, F. Bellotti, L. Lazzaroni, M. Fresta, A. Pighetti, A. Abedinia, N. Yoonesi, D. P. Pau

Journal of Systems Architecture · Elsevier · 2026

A fully local speech-to-text → language-model → text-to-speech pipeline (Moonshine, Piper, and four quantised LMs from 1.2B to 8B parameters), deployed on an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin, a Raspberry Pi 5 and an STM32MP257F-EV1, and characterised on thermostat and washing-machine use cases.

Article

Enhancing classification with semi-supervised deep learning using distance-based sample weights

A. Abedinia, S. Tabakhi, V. Seydi

10th Int. Conf. on Machine Learning Technologies (ICMLT) · IEEE · Helsinki, 2025 · oral

Weights training samples by their distance to the test distribution, down-weighting noisy and out-of-distribution examples. Helps most when labels are few or classes are imbalanced — 2–7 absolute points across benchmarks in the low-label regime.

Building semi-supervised decision trees with semi-CART algorithm

A. Abedinia, V. Seydi

International Journal of Machine Learning and Cybernetics · Springer · 15(10), 4493–4510, 2024 34 citations

Extends CART so unlabeled data shapes the split criterion, through sample weights derived from the distance between labeled and unlabeled instances. Grew out of my MSc thesis on optimising XGBoost for semi-supervised datasets.
